Environmental Monitoring & Risk Prediction

Environmental Monitoring & Risk Prediction

IoT sensors track temperature, humidity, ammonia (NH₃) and CO₂ while a vision module reads flock crowd density and activity.

Poultry Disease Detection

A dual-stream deep learning model analyses fecal images and visible body symptoms (eyes, comb, feathers) captured on a smartphone, classifying Coccidiosis, Salmonella, Newcastle Disease, Infectious Bronchitis or Healthy.

Egg Quality & Yield Prediction

A low-cost grading rig with a conveyor belt, load-cell weighing (HX711) and a camera-based CNN sorts every egg by size and cleanliness into six grades, from Jumbo-Clean to Small-Dirty, on a Raspberry Pi 4.

Snake & Scorpion Detection

Near-Infrared imaging improves snake visibility and UV fluorescence reveals scorpions in low-light sheds, while a YOLO model detects intruders in real time.

Our Mission

SENSE.
DETECT.
PROTECT.

Farm Pulse brings environmental sensing, disease detection, automated egg grading and intruder detection into one system, so small and medium-scale poultry farmers rely less on manual checks and can act on problems sooner.
