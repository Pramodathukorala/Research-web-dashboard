01 · Literature Survey

What existing research tells us

Poultry farming is a major agricultural sector in Sri Lanka, especially for small and medium-scale farmers. Research on smart poultry farming spans several overlapping areas:

Precision livestock farming

Sensing, data processing and automation let farmers watch animals continuously instead of relying on periodic manual inspection. [1]

Environmental monitoring

IoT sensors for temperature, humidity, ammonia and CO₂ are widely studied, but most deployments stop at threshold alerts without predictive analytics. [2]

Disease detection

Deep learning on poultry fecal images can flag diseases, yet most work uses a single image modality and ignores visible body symptoms. [3]

Automated egg grading

Weight- and vision-based egg inspection is established, but low-cost systems that grade size and cleanliness together are rare. [4]

Intruder detection

Snake and scorpion detection has been studied outdoors and in urban settings, not in dim, cluttered, enclosed poultry sheds. [5]

Computer vision & XAI

Object detectors such as YOLO and explainability methods such as Grad-CAM make vision models faster and easier for farmers to trust. [6]

02 · Research Gap

Where current solutions fall short

Existing tools tend to solve one narrow problem in isolation, and often under conditions that do not match a real enclosed poultry shed.

Existing approaches
  • Environmental monitoring shows raw readings only, with no risk classification or forecasting
  • Disease models analyse a single image type (fecal images) and give no explanation
  • Egg grading is manual, or handles size and cleanliness separately
  • Snake / scorpion detectors are built for outdoor or urban scenes
Farm Pulse approach
  • Sensor + vision data classified into normal / warning / high risk, with predictive models
  • Dual-stream fecal + body-symptom analysis with Grad-CAM explanations, offline on Android
  • Combined weight + CNN cleanliness grading into six categories
  • NIR + UV fluorescence imaging with YOLO, built for low-light enclosed sheds
03 · Research Problem

Why poultry farms need smarter monitoring

  1. Rapidly changing shed conditions — temperature, humidity, NH₃ and CO₂ fluctuate quickly and cause heat stress, disease and mortality when unmanaged.
  2. Late disease detection — affordable early-stage diagnosis tools are scarce for small and medium-scale farmers.
  3. Manual egg grading — slow, inconsistent and error-prone, and unable to keep pace with continuous laying.
  4. Dangerous intruders — snakes and scorpions destroy eggs and chicks, stress flocks and endanger workers.
  5. Fragmented monitoring — separate, manual tasks make it hard to get one clear view of the farm.
04 · Objectives

What we set out to achieve

Main Objective

To develop an AI-powered smart poultry farm monitoring and management system that supports environmental risk prediction, early disease detection, automated egg grading and farm safety.

  • 01Build a multi-parameter IoT sensing and vision module that classifies and predicts poultry-house environmental risk.
  • 02Detect common chicken diseases from fecal and body-symptom images, offline on Android, with explainable results.
  • 03Grade eggs automatically by weight-based size and CNN-based cleanliness for real-time sorting.
  • 04Detect snakes and scorpions in real time using NIR / UV imaging, YOLO and a Flock Unrest Index.
  • 05Deliver alerts, trends and recommendations to farmers through a mobile application.
05 · Methodology

How the research is carried out

All components follow a shared pipeline, adapted to each component's data and goals.

  1. Data Collection

    Sensor readings, farm images and video

  2. Pre-processing

    Cleaning, labelling and feature preparation

  3. AI Analysis

    Classification, detection and prediction models

  4. Evaluation

    Accuracy and field validation

  5. Farmer Output

    Alerts, grades and reports in the app

Environmental Monitoring & Risk Prediction

IoT sensors track temperature, humidity, ammonia (NH₃) and CO₂ while a vision module reads flock crowd density and activity. Machine learning classifies house conditions as normal, warning or high risk and forecasts environmental risk from historical trends.

Poultry Disease Detection

A dual-stream deep learning model analyses fecal images and visible body symptoms (eyes, comb, feathers) captured on a smartphone, classifying Coccidiosis, Salmonella, Newcastle Disease, Infectious Bronchitis or Healthy. Grad-CAM explains each prediction, and the model runs fully offline on Android.

Egg Quality & Yield Prediction

A low-cost grading rig with a conveyor belt, load-cell weighing (HX711) and a camera-based CNN sorts every egg by size and cleanliness into six grades, from Jumbo-Clean to Small-Dirty, on a Raspberry Pi 4.

Snake & Scorpion Detection

Near-Infrared imaging improves snake visibility and UV fluorescence reveals scorpions in low-light sheds, while a YOLO model detects intruders in real time. A Flock Unrest Index adds a second, behaviour-based warning.
